Athar is a calm daily companion for Islamic remembrance (adhkar) and prayer times.

No ads. No subscriptions. No accounts. No internet required.


ADHKAR

108 adhkar across 10 collections: morning, evening, before sleep, on waking, after prayer, for distress and worry, seeking forgiveness, sending peace upon the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم, the lasting good deeds, and adhkar for the day and night.

Every text is fully vowelled (tashkeel) so it reads correctly, and carries its source (Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah, Ahmad) along with its narrated virtue.

Tap the counter to count, swipe for the next dhikr. A progress bar shows where you are, and a quiet celebration when you finish.


PRAYER TIMES

The five prayers plus sunrise, computed astronomically on your device — no internet, no servers.

- Live countdown to the next prayer
- An alert when each time enters
- Six calculation methods: Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, Egyptian, Karachi, ISNA, Dubai
- Both Asr methods: standard and Hanafi
- Use your current location, or pick from 35 cities


TASBIH

A counter with 33 / 100 / 500 / 1000 targets, eight ready phrases, gentle haptics on each count, and completed-round tracking.


WIDGETS

For the Home Screen and the Lock Screen:
- A dhikr that refreshes through the day
- The next prayer and time remaining
- Your daily streak and total count


REMINDERS

Morning and evening adhkar reminders at times you choose, plus prayer-time alerts. All local notifications from your own device — no servers, no push.


YOUR PRIVACY

We collect nothing. Nothing at all.

No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no third-party SDKs, and no servers of our own. Everything — your counts, your stats, your location — stays on your device and is erased when you delete the app.

Location access is entirely optional, and the prayer-time calculation runs on your device. The only network request the app can make is an optional lookup to Apple's geocoding service to turn your coordinates into a city name, and only if you enable location.

The interface is in Arabic.


WHY "ATHAR"?

Athar means a trace, a lasting mark. Remembrance does not stop with you — like a drop falling into still water, its circles keep widening.

The app is free for everyone and will stay that way.


Note: prayer times are computed astronomically and may differ by a few minutes from your local mosque's calendar. When they differ, follow your mosque.
